EPS firmware: every version compared

Four firmwares, one table each: what they do, how each one handles 17 different corruptions of the network chip, how they recover when the link, the gateway or the receiver fails, how many network-standard requirements they meet, and how they behave day to day. Everything was measured on the same real EPS box with the same pre-registered tools; where a version wasn't tested, the table says so.

Status 2026-10-12 · unit 3 (real EPS box), direct cable to a PC · v2.2 field firmware, v3.1 (2026-10-10), v3.2 (2026-10-11), v3.3 (2026-10-12 overnight) · every trial reported

På dansk: Alle versioner sammenlignet · Hvorfor v3.3 (kort)

In one paragraph

v3.3 is the most robust of the four on every measurement we have, and it is the one we recommend as official. v2.2 needs a power cycle after most network faults; v3.1 recovers from many but dies on four chip faults including the one that matches the field symptom; v3.2 recovers from those but is dead on a PHY held in reset and its receiver check stops testing anything once the gateway has answered once. v3.3 recovers from all 17 faults, really tests its receiver every minute, and records the supply voltage at every chip fault. None of them is field-proven yet, and the leading field trigger (a supply sag) is still to be tested by hand.

What each version does

Pins, outputs, sequence timing, inputs, the LED, the TCP control protocol and the stored settings are the same in all four; only the network layer differs. v3.1 → v3.2 → v3.3 are small steps on the same rewritten driver.

What these tables don't show

Measured

  • All four firmwares on the same real box (10 V supply, six SSRs, no loads), same harness, three trials per fault.
  • v3.3 overnight 2026-10-12: 51 fault-matrix trials, 50 USB-reset start-ups, the full regression suite, link-down, gateway-gone and deaf-receiver tests.
  • The box's brown-out level (2.7 V) and supply (≈ 5.1 V on USB), read by v3.3 itself.
  • Twice in 40 chip-reset trials on v3.3 the network chip came back deaf after its re-init (38 s and ~3.5 min), then recovered by itself; one episode captured (transmitting, answering nothing). v3.1: 0 in 30.

Not tested yet

  • Supply sags and slow power-up. The leading hypothesis for the field dropouts (the box reports a 2.7 V brown-out level). Needs the USB cable out to really cut power, so it needs hands-on time.
  • Real loads switched by the SSRs, multi-day operation and other switch brands.
  • v3.3 acting on a real deaf receiver. The two real episodes on this box cleared by themselves before the check acted; the check itself is tested by emulation.
  • A W5500 that stops answering on SPI. No firmware on this board can reset it (no reset pin wired); it needs a hardware change.
  • Field rates: one box, three trials per fault. These are behaviours, not failure rates.
