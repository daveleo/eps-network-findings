Why EPS units drop off the network, and what firmware 3.1 changes

EPS units on firmware v2.2 sometimes stop answering on the network until their power is cycled. These documents describe the bench investigation (three days, three units, a NETGEAR M4250 switch), what it found, the rewritten firmware (3.0) and the current final firmware 3.1, including a network-standards comparison with v2.2.
