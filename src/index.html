Expromo · EPS power sequencer

Why EPS units drop off the network, and the firmware that fixes it

EPS units on firmware v2.2 sometimes stop answering on the network until their power is cycled. These documents describe the bench investigation (three units, a NETGEAR M4250 switch and a real EPS box), a full code analysis, what was found, the rewritten firmware, and the case for making v3.3 the official firmware. A bench study (2026-10-27/28) has now reproduced the fault from a supply sag, traced it to a slow-rising supply edge, and shown it needs a hardware fix — start there.

Supply sag is the trigger — and it needs a hardware fix

The field fault reproduced on the bench: a slow-rising supply edge wedges the Ethernet chip until a power cycle.
